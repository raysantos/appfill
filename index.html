AppFill is a Chrome extension that fills job applications from one profile, outlines anything it can't answer, and keeps every detail on your own computer.

Free · Chrome and Edge on Windows or Mac · 28 KB · No account

Your details never leave your computer

  • lockNo account, no sync, no server. Your profile lives in Chrome's local storage, and the code makes no network requests.
  • ads_clickOnly the form you point at. Ads, chat widgets and other frames on the page are never filled.
  • visibility_offTrap fields stay empty. Hidden inputs that ask for your details are skipped, and AppFill tells you they're there.
  • shieldPrivate answers aren't remembered. Social Security, date of birth, bank, card, license and passport fields are never saved.

warning What's a trap field? An input a page makes invisible: see-through, a few pixels wide, or pushed off the screen. Some sites use them to catch spam bots; others use them to collect details you never see. AppFill never fills them.

Install in two minutes

  1. Download and unzip. Right-click appfill.zip and choose Extract All. Keep the folder somewhere permanent, like Documents, because Chrome loads it from there.
  2. Open the extensions page. Type chrome://extensions into the address bar. In Edge it's edge://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode. The switch is at the top right of the page.
  4. Load it. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped appfill folder.
  5. Add your details. The settings page opens. Fill in what you want AppFill to use. It saves as you type.

Updating? Replace the files in the same folder, then click the reload arrow on AppFill's card in chrome://extensions.
